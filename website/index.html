One folder, two ways to rotate

Mode 1

Shuffle

Every rotation brings a random pick from your collection. Great for big wallpaper folders where you never want the same picture twice in a row.

Mode 2

Sequential

Walks through your folder in order, one image at a time. Ideal for curated packs, photo trips, or collections with a story to tell.

Features

Everything you need from a wallpaper rotator — and nothing you don't.

Flexible interval

Set the rotation cadence anywhere from 1 minute to 24 hours. Your desktop, your pace.

Shuffle or sequential

Random surprise or ordered walk-through. Switch modes any time with one click.

Manual skip & pause

Not feeling today's pick? Skip to the next wallpaper instantly, or pause and keep it.

Start with Windows

Toggle autostart on and rotation begins at boot — no clicks needed after that.

Runs in the tray

Close the window and it keeps going in the background, with a quick tray menu.

Private by design

No network access, no telemetry. Your photos never leave your machine.

Privacy-friendly: no network, no telemetry

Your photos stay yours.

ShufflePaper works entirely offline. It reads images from the folder you pick and sets your wallpaper — that's all. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is tracked, and the settings file lives on your own disk.
